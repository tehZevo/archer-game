# Platforms

You move through the game by **climbing floors** — single-screen floors, one after another, getting higher as you go.

A typical floor is **quick** — about a minute, a few monsters to fight, then you're climbing to the next one. A full climb is **medium, about 30-50 floors** from bottom to top.

Floor layouts are **randomized per run** (seeded by things like the time and channel), so each climb is fresh, yet **seeded so everyone on the same channel sees the same floor**. **Towns are never randomized** — they're fixed.

When you go down (your health runs out), you **restart in town** at the bottom of the climb.
