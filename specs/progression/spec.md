# Progression

The player starts at Lv.1 as an Archers-in-training with base stats from the level table. Killing mobs grants EXP; the EXP needed to the next level grows with level. Each level-up raises the stat baselines and grants 1 AP; every 5th level also grants 1 SP. A short banner announces the level and the HUD shows unspent points. Points are spent from the pause menu whenever the player likes: AP goes to DEX, VIT, SPI or LUK; SP learns a newly available skill, takes a slot upgrade (Twin Shot to Shell Barrage or Backstep Shot, Sky Toss to Vault Shot, Skyfall Volley to Sky Assault; where a slot has two branches, taking one locks the other out), or raises a learned skill's rank. The player starts with about 100 HP and 50 MP; both pools grow by about 5% per level. Damage starts low: at Lv.1 a Garden Snail takes a few basic attacks to kill, and WAR grows in step with mob HP.

Stats are derived, not stored per-point: the level table supplies base values and spent AP adds on top. Each VIT point adds 1% bonus max HP. EXP to next level and current level are shown in the HUD. Esc (or the touch pause button) opens a pause menu that freezes the game. It shows the current stats (level, HP, MP, attack, crit, EXP) above two columns: core stats with spent points (spend AP here), and every known or newly learnable skill and passive with its rank (spend SP here). Arrow keys move between rows and columns, Z spends on the selected row, and a line at the bottom describes what the selection does; on touch, tap a row to select it and tap again to spend. Esc again resumes. Progress persists across floors within a run; the tower loops every 10 floors via the town.

Progress is saved in the browser (localStorage): level, EXP, unspent AP/SP, core stats, skill ranks (including the upgrade branch taken) and the current floor. It autosaves every 5 seconds while playing and whenever the page is hidden or closed. On reload the menu already shows your level, and Play resumes on the saved floor with full HP/MP. A corrupt or old-version save is ignored, and out-of-range values are clamped. "Reset progress" in the menu (with a confirm) erases the save.
