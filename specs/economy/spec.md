# Economy

Coins are the currency. A defeated enemy drops a pile of coins that pops up and falls onto the platform beneath it; a pile is worth more on higher floors (by the enemy's level: between level + 1 and 2 × level + 2 coins) and shows as one to three stacked coins by its value. Stand beside a pile and press **X** to pick it up (piles within about a body-width of you, at your feet). Uncollected piles blink in their last 5 seconds and vanish after 30; leaving the floor leaves them behind. Coins and drops are local to each player, like enemies.

Potions restore 40% of max HP or MP: **A** drinks an HP potion and **S** an MP potion (when you have one and aren't already full), with a short cooldown between drinks. Up to 99 of each can be carried.

Every town has a shopkeeper on the plaza deck. Standing beside them shows "UP: SHOP"; pressing Up opens the shop window, which holds the world still while you browse: Up/Down pick an item, Z or Enter buys one (HP potion 30 coins, MP potion 30 coins; prices in red when you can't afford them), and Esc or X closes it.

The HUD shows your coins and potions under your level and place ("123G  A:HP 3  S:MP 2"). Coins and potions are saved with your progress; an older save without them starts with none. On touch screens, X, A and S have their own buttons.
