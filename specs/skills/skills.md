# Skills

Skills are the tools of the combo. You mix them freely; cooldowns and use conditions shape what's available.

## Toolkit size

Your toolkit **starts small and grows**. At level 1 you have just a few skills; by level 60+ you're juggling 8 or more. The kit evolves as you climb, so the combo deepens over the run.

## Getting stronger

You get stronger by **gaining levels and spending points to learn new skills and rank up existing ones**. Your power comes from the skills you pick and rank up.
