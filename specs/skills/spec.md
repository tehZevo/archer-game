# Skills

The attack keys are Z (main), Q, W, E and R, plus movement keys. Some slots upgrade in place when a stronger skill unlocks:

- **Z:** Twin Shot (Lv.1) raises the two crossbows for a tenth of a second, then hits the two closest enemies in range ahead twice each (no projectile) with a small recoil, and follows through (about 0.4s in all); from Lv.30 it branches: upgrade it to Shell Barrage (a stunning burst) or to Backstep Shot (hop backward along the ground while still firing forward, for hit-and-run). Taking one branch locks out the other.
- **Q:** Sky Toss (known from the start) slides the player forward, scooping up the enemies in the way (and any just behind you) and pushing them along in front, standing on their own platform; nothing is launched during the slide. The strike lands when the slide ends, hitting the carried enemies and those around where you stopped and launching them airborne; it hits at most 6 enemies at every rank. The slide never carries you off a platform: it stops at the edge, and the strike lands there. (Down + Q strikes in place, no slide.) From Lv.20 it branches: upgrade it to Vault Shot (leap backward into the air while launching the enemies ahead) or to Rising Arrow (launch the enemies ahead and jump straight up with them, rising together); taking one locks the other out. Both open the same link window, so W can follow from the air.
- **W:** the link skill. Skyfall Volley (pickable from Lv.5) fires arrows at the launched enemies; it can be upgraded to Sky Assault from Lv.15, which warps the player straight up, well above the launched enemies (half again past the highest one; no horizontal movement), hovers there for about half a second striking every enemy still in the air, then drops with normal gravity.
- **E:** Plunging Volley (Lv.10, mid-air only) gives you a small upward bump (no hover: you keep drifting and fall right after) and rains arrows straight down into a narrow column beneath you, hitting every enemy in it (not ones off to the side) and setting them burning for a few seconds (a damage-over-time that stacks with repeat casts). Its cooldown is short (0.25s). **R:** Piercing Arrow (Lv.25) is a heavy finisher on a long cooldown: one arrow down the line ahead that hits every enemy it passes through.
- **C:** Jump keeps running momentum. **D:** Glide is the air move.

Link skills can only be used right after Sky Toss, once per Sky Toss: W, or the basic attack (Z), during the link window fires the link skill. A link pressed while Sky Toss is still playing is queued and fires as soon as Sky Toss finishes. A link bypasses cooldown and use time and stacks Fervor.

Skills have at most 3 ranks, and each rank is a big, readable jump (more damage, an extra hit or an extra target) rather than a small percentage. Jump and Glide are unranked. New skills, slot upgrades and ranks are all bought with SP, earned every 5 levels and spent from the pause menu (see progression); an upgrade replaces the base skill on its key and keeps its rank, so it's never a step back. There are about 20 SP by the level cap, a few short of maxing everything, so players make some real choices without facing a long list.

Passives have no key and are learned and ranked through the same picks: Precision raises crit chance, Power Surge raises all damage, and Fervor makes each Fervor stack worth more.

Attack skills have a short use time during which no other skill or movement input works; a link skill bypasses it. Jump and Glide have no use time.

A skill's use time is the **whole time it commits the player**: every phase of the move — the leap, the hover, the slide, the arrows flying out and landing — fits inside it, and a skill linked off it fires at the end of it. One number per skill, so raising it slows the entire move and lowering it speeds the entire move up.

Every active skill costs MP, scaled by job tier: **1st Job 10–50 MP, 2nd Job 50–100 MP, 3rd Job 100–200 MP, 4th Job 200–400 MP**. Within a tier's band, cost scales with impact: longer cooldown and more power/damage cost more MP (Trefoil Tempest's 10s cooldown and 6-hit sweep sits near the top of its band, the no-cooldown main attacks near the bottom). Passives cost no MP.

Using a skill doesn't announce its name on screen.

## Unlock grouping

Skills unlock in four job tiers, each covering a level range: **I Archer (1–10)**, **II Sky Archer (10–30)**, **III Mystic Archer (30–60)**, **IV Adele (60–100)**. A skill belongs to exactly one tier and becomes learnable at that tier's base level; you acquire it by spending SP on it in the pause menu.

Reaching Lv.10, 30 or 60 only makes you eligible for the next job: you take it by visiting the **Sage** (an NPC in every town), which unlocks that job's skills and raises your max HP and MP: +5% at the 2nd job, +10% more at the 3rd, +15% more at the 4th (+30% in all). Until then the job's skills show "SEE THE SAGE".

- **Job I — Archer (Lv.1–10):** the starter kit, available from character creation.
- **Job II — Sky Archer (Lv.10–30):** the second job's wave of skills.
- **Job III — Mystic Archer (Lv.30–60):** the third job's group (with the job's SP bonus).
- **Job IV — Adele (Lv.60–100):** the fourth job's group (with the job's SP bonus).

Which skills sit in each group is deliberately left open for now and will be decided as the build evolves; what's fixed is the cadence — four unlock moments, one per job tier. Skill upgrades (new forms, skills that build on another skill) are generally not unlockable in the same group as the skill they build on, so mastering a base skill and discovering its replacement are separate moments.

SP is awarded to fund each group as it opens, sized so the skills in a group are affordable when it unlocks, with enough total SP to max most (not all) of the tree by the level cap.
