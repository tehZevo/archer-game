# Skills

The attack keys are Z (main), Q, W, E and R, plus movement keys. Some slots upgrade in place when a stronger skill unlocks:

- **Z:** Twin Shot (Lv.1) draws the bow for a tenth of a second, then hits the two closest enemies in range ahead twice each (no projectile) with a small recoil, and follows through (about 0.4s in all); from Lv.30 it branches: upgrade it to Shell Barrage (a stunning burst) or to Backstep Shot (hop backward along the ground while still firing forward, for hit-and-run). Taking one branch locks out the other.
- **Q:** Sky Toss (known from the start) slides the player forward, scooping up the enemies in the way (and any just behind you) and pushing them along in front, standing on their own platform; nothing is launched during the slide. The strike lands when the slide ends, hitting the carried enemies and those around where you stopped and launching them airborne; it hits at most 6 enemies at every rank. The slide never carries you off a platform: it stops at the edge, and the strike lands there. (Down + Q strikes in place, no slide.) From Lv.20 it branches: upgrade it to Vault Shot (leap backward into the air while launching the enemies ahead) or to Rising Arrow (launch the enemies ahead and jump straight up with them, rising together); taking one locks the other out. Both open the same link window, so W can follow from the air.
- **W:** the link skill. Skyfall Volley (pickable from Lv.5) fires arrows at the launched enemies; it can be upgraded to Sky Assault from Lv.15, which warps the player straight up, well above the launched enemies (half again past the highest one; no horizontal movement), hovers there for about half a second striking every enemy still in the air, then drops with normal gravity.
- **E:** Plunging Volley (Lv.10, mid-air only) gives you a small upward bump (no hover: you keep drifting and fall right after) and rains arrows straight down into a narrow column beneath you, hitting every enemy in it (not ones off to the side). Its cooldown is short (0.25s). **R:** Piercing Arrow (Lv.25) is a heavy finisher on a long cooldown: one arrow down the line ahead that hits every enemy it passes through.
- **C:** Jump keeps running momentum. **D:** Glide is the air move.

Link skills can only be used right after Sky Toss, once per Sky Toss: W, or the basic attack (Z), during the link window fires the link skill. A link pressed while Sky Toss is still playing is queued and fires as soon as Sky Toss finishes. A link bypasses cooldown and use time and stacks Fervor.

Skills have at most 3 ranks, and each rank is a big, readable jump (more damage, an extra hit or an extra target) rather than a small percentage. Jump and Glide are unranked. New skills, slot upgrades and ranks are all bought with SP, earned every 5 levels and spent from the pause menu (see progression); an upgrade replaces the base skill on its key and keeps its rank, so it's never a step back. There are about 20 SP by the level cap, a few short of maxing everything, so players make some real choices without facing a long list.

Passives have no key and are learned and ranked through the same picks: Precision raises crit chance, Power Surge raises all damage, and Fervor makes each Fervor stack worth more.

Attack skills have a short use time during which no other skill or movement input works; a link skill bypasses it. Jump and Glide have no use time.

Using a skill doesn't announce its name on screen.

## Unlock grouping (planned)

All skills unlock at one of four levels, matching the job ladder: **1, 10, 30, 60**.

- **Lv.1 — Basics:** Twin Shot (Z, free at rank 1); Jump (C, free); Glide (D); Precision (passive).
- **Lv.10 — Aerial Combo:** Sky Toss (Q) with its Vault Shot / Rising Arrow branch; Skyfall Volley (W) upgradable to Sky Assault; Midair Spin (its sweep, requiring Sky Assault); Air Dive (its plunge, requiring Sky Toss); Agile Climbing; Fervor (passive).
- **Lv.30 — Ground Burst:** Skyfall Volley sits with Sky Toss as its link; the movement skills arrive as two tiers: air (Jump + Glide at 1), then climb (Agile Climbing at 10).
- **Lv.30 — Ground Burst:** Spike Lift (E) and its air form Plunging Volley; Haste (passive); Echo Shot (passive); the Z-slot upgrade Shell Barrage.
- **Lv.60 — Storm:** Electric Blitz; Tempest (R); Power Surge (passive).

Skill upgrades — the new forms (Shell Barrage, Sky Assault, Vault Shot, Rising Arrow) and the skills that build on another skill (Midair Spin on Sky Assault, Air Dive on Sky Toss, Plunging Volley on Spike Lift) — are generally **not** unlockable in the same job as the skill they build on: the base skill and its upgrade live in different groups, so mastering the base and discovering its replacement are separate moments in the build.

SP is awarded to fund each group as it opens (roughly 10 at Lv.1, 14 at Lv.10, 16+5 at Lv.30, 14+5 at Lv.60 — about 59 total against the ~52 needed to max everything), leaving a little choice pressure without punishing experimentation.
